About Us

Adesol Collections is Osogbo's trusted name in fashion and creative design. Located in the heart of Power Line area, we specialize in transforming fabric into masterpieces that reflect your unique personality and style. Our skilled designers combine traditional Nigerian aesthetics with modern fashion trends to create outfits that make you stand out. Whether you need native attire for special occasions, contemporary corporate wear, or creative custom designs, we deliver excellence in every stitch. At Adesol Collections, we don't just make clothes – we craft confidence.

Our Services

Bespoke Fashion Design

Custom-made outfits tailored to your exact measurements and style preferences. From traditional aso-ebi to modern cocktail dresses, we bring your vision to life.

Native & Traditional Wear

Exquisite Nigerian traditional attire including agbada, iro and buba, kaftan, and senator styles for weddings, parties, and cultural events.

Corporate & Casual Wear

Professional workwear and everyday casual outfits that combine comfort with style for the modern Nigerian professional.

Bridal & Special Occasions

Stunning bridal gowns, bridesmaid dresses, and special event outfits that make your big day unforgettable.

Fashion Alterations

Expert alterations and adjustments to ensure your garments fit perfectly and flatter your body shape.

Creative Fashion Consulting

Personal styling sessions and wardrobe consultations to help you discover and refine your unique fashion identity.
